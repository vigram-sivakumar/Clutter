I did warn you this one’s boring. But a good quote earns its place, so here we go
## Making a quote
- Start a line with `> or "` and a space
- Press `Enter` and the next line stays inside the quote
- Press `Enter` on an empty line to step back out
" Like this. Someone said something smart, and now it lives in your notes #Ideas
## A little more
- Need a quote inside a quote? Use `>>`
>>For the conversations that go a few levels deep
- Everything else works inside a quote too, **bold**, ==highlights==, [[Welcome to Clutter/Wiki links & embeds|links]], even lists
**Tip:** I use quotes for side notes, the kind of thing you’d scribble in the margin. You’ve probably noticed by now #Tip

" The `> or "` hides once you move away, so all you see is the line on the left
