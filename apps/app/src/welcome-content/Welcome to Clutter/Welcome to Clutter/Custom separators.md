Last one, and my little indulgence. A plain line does the job, but sometimes a page deserves a bit of flair
## Pick a style
Type it on its own line and it turns into a divider

- `---` for a plain line
---
- `~---~` for a wavy one
~---~
- `=---=` for a double line
=---=
- `.---.` for a dotted one
.---.
## Give it a label
Put some words in the middle of any style
- `--- Chapter 1 ---`
- `~--- Somewhere in between ---~`
**Tip:** I use labels to split a long note into chapters. It reads like a journal that way #Tip

" Leave an empty line above a plain `---`. Without it, the line above turns into a heading. Not my fault, I promise, that’s just how Markdown works
