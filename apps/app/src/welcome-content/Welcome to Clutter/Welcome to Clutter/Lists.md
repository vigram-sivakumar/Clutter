Lists are how most of my thinking starts, so let’s get them right
Start a line with a symbol and a space, and Clutter turns it into a list. Hit `Enter` and the next item is ready and waiting
## The three kinds
- Start with `-`, `*` or `+` for a simple bullet list
1. Type `1.` for a numbered list
2. Add, remove or nest items, and Clutter keeps the numbers in order for you
😎 Or start with any emoji for a list with a little personality
🫰🏽 Pick whichever emoji fits the mood
## Moving around
- Press `Tab` to nest an item under the one above
	- Like this
		- Go as deep as you like
- Press `⇧ Tab` to bring it back out
- Press `Enter` on an empty item to finish the list
- Need a second line inside the same item? Press `⇧ Enter`
**Tip:** press `Delete` at the start of an item to remove its bullet or number and keep the text #Tip

>Want checkboxes? Type `[]` and a space at the start of a line. There’s more on that in [[Welcome to Clutter/Tasks & date mentions]]
