This is my favourite part. Notes on their own are fine, but linked together they start to feel like a second brain
## Linking to a note
- Type `[[` and start typing a note’s name. Clutter suggests matches as you go
- Click the link to jump straight to that note
- Linking to a note that doesn’t exist yet? Click it and Clutter creates it for you
## Making it your own
- Show different text with `|`, like `[[Get started|start here]]`
- Point to a heading inside a note with `#`, like `[[Get started#Find your way around]]`. Clutter suggests the headings too
- Link to a folder with a `/` at the end, like `[[Daily Notes/]]`
## Embeds
Add `!` in front of a link and the thing shows up right inside your note, not just a link to it
- `![[photo.png]]` for an image
- `![[report.pdf]]` for a PDF
- `![[Another note]]` to show a whole note inside this one
**Tip:** changed your mind about an embedded note? Its menu can turn it back into a plain link #Tip

>Rename a note, or even a heading, and every link pointing to it updates on its own. Nothing breaks
